A focused toolkit

Designed to stay
out of your way.

Vāk keeps desktop dictation simple: trigger dictation with a keypress, speak naturally, and return text directly to the app where you are already working.

⌨

Hotkey-Based Activation

Activate Vāk instantly with a global hotkey from any window. Press and hold to dictate, release to finish transcription and insert text.

◉

Local Speech Processing

Transcription runs completely on-device using a downloaded Whisper model. Audio is not sent to external cloud speech-recognition services.

↳

Direct Text Injection

Vāk automatically injects transcribed text directly where your cursor lives via the clipboard, with configurable clipboard restoration.

〰

Automatic Speech Detection

Integrated voice-activity detection (VAD) monitors vocal input during recording sessions, filtering non-speech sound for cleaner transcription.

⚙

Whisper Model Choices

V1 supports tiny.en, base.en, and small.en Whisper models, giving you control over transcription accuracy and hardware performance.

🖥

Windows Desktop Integration

Engineered for Windows 10 and 11 as a native background utility. Works seamlessly across code editors, browsers, chat apps, and note tools.

A clear note about privacy

Local transcription means speech is transcribed on your computer. During inference, Vāk temporarily creates local audio files, and diagnostic logs may contain transcript text. We believe in complete transparency about how local data is handled.
