A focused toolkit

Designed to stay
out of your way.

Vāk keeps the workflow simple: trigger dictation, speak, and return text to the app where you were already working.

⌨

Hotkey-first workflow

Activate Vāk with its configured global hotkey. Recording ends when you release it or the session reaches its time limit.

◉

On-device recognition

Vāk uses a Whisper model on your computer. Audio is not sent to a cloud speech-recognition service by its transcription pipeline.

↳

Text at the cursor

Vāk uses the clipboard and a paste action to insert text into the active application. Clipboard restoration can be configured.

▤

Recent history

History can be disabled, and entries can be deleted. Diagnostic logs are separate from history.

⚙

English model choices

V1 supports tiny.en, base.en, and small.en Whisper models. Larger models need more storage and processing resources.

◷

Focused sessions

Vāk records on demand while you hold the configured hotkey. It is not designed as an always-on recorder.

A clear note about privacy

Local transcription does not mean no local data is written. Vāk V1 temporarily writes audio to a WAV file during inference, and diagnostic logs can include transcript text even when history is disabled.
