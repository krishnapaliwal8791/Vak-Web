⌨

Dictation doesn’t start

Check that Vāk is running in the background, your configured hotkey is pressed, and Windows allows microphone access for desktop apps in Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone.

↳

Text doesn’t appear

Ensure the target application has active focus and the cursor is placed in an editable text field. Vāk inserts text through the clipboard followed by a paste keystroke.

◉

Transcription is slow

Speech recognition runs 100% locally on your computer. Model size (tiny.en, base.en, or small.en) and your computer's CPU/GPU resources affect processing speed.

⚙

Model download failed

Verify your internet connection and available disk space, then retry downloading your chosen Whisper model directly within Vāk’s setup or settings screen.
