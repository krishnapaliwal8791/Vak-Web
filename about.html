The idea behind Vāk

Speech should fit
your workflow.

Vāk is a focused Windows desktop utility built around one idea: speak a thought and have it appear directly where you are already typing.

One focused job, done simply.

Vāk is designed as a control panel for voice dictation, not a bloated chatbot or an isolated writing application. The system-wide hotkey lets you start a dictation session from any other app; Vāk transcribes audio locally using Whisper models and inserts the resulting text right at your cursor.

Vāk is officially released and publicly available on the Microsoft Store for Windows 10 and 11. The goal is to make speaking into text feel like a natural, effortless extension of your keyboard.
